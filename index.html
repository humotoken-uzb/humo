Frequently asked questions

Why is HUMO Token needed?

HUMO Token was created to offer the market a reliable, stable, and transparent financial instrument, fully denominated in US dollars and backed by real reserves.

The project's goal is to develop regulated and secure crypto infrastructure on Solana, accessible to both businesses and individuals.

How is HUMO Token regulated?

HUMO Token is issued and circulated under the rules of the Humo token ecosystem and the Asterium ecosystem.

The stable token fully complies with requirements and operates within the legal framework of Humo token.

The issuance is registered in the crypto-asset registry; rights are recorded by the licensed Asterium crypto-depository.

What backs HUMO Token?

HUMO Token is fully backed by reserves and denominated in US dollars.

The backing corresponds to the number of issued stable tokens and undergoes regular checks in accordance with Humo token regulations.

This guarantees the stability and transparency of HUMO Token for all users.

What is the price of HUMO Token?

The nominal value of HUMO Token is fixed: 1 HUMO Token = $0.0001. At this ratio, the issuer issues and redeems stable tokens.

This rate is fixed and unchanging — it does not depend on market fluctuations, external or internal factors, and remains stable over time.

Who issues HUMO Token?

The stable token is issued by HUMO CRYPTO — owner of HUMO PAY (the processing center of the Asterium ecosystem), officially registered in Humo token.
